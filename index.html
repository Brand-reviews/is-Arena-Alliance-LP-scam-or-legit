Does Arena Alliance LP Have a Financial Trading Licence?

Corporate registration and financial-services authorization are two entirely different things.

Arena Alliance LP appears in the UK Companies House register as a limited partnership. However, this registration alone does not establish that it is authorized to offer forex, CFD trading, cryptocurrency investment management or other regulated financial services.

During our research, we could not establish a verifiable financial-services licence covering the investment services advertised under the Arena Alliance LP name.

Prospective investors should independently check the relevant regulatory databases, including:

  • UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)
  • Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA)
  • Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA)
Important regulatory distinction:

A company registration certificate, an attractive website, an SSL security certificate or an office address does not automatically establish authorization to provide investment services.

Always verify the exact legal entity, licence number, permitted activities and applicable investor protections.

What If Arena Alliance LP Promises Guaranteed Profits?

Beware of Investment Recovery Scams

Investors who experience difficulties recovering money from online trading platforms can become targets of secondary scams.

Some individuals may claim to be professional recovery agents, blockchain investigators or financial lawyers capable of retrieving lost funds.

They may request advance payments, administrative charges or supposed government taxes before beginning the recovery process.

Recovery scam warning:

Never trust anyone promising guaranteed recovery of lost investment funds. Independently verify the credentials of any lawyer, investigator or recovery service before engaging them.
